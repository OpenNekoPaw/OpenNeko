## Why

Desktop currently exposes product areas through inconsistent top-level compositions. A single Window needs one stable Shell and Workbench so navigation changes visible owner-qualified surfaces without creating parallel application or lifecycle authorities.

## What Changes

- Compose entry, Project, management, and Settings scenes through one Window-level Workbench and Primary Sidebar.
- Mount only current or explicitly split business Roots while preserving durable records and protected background work.
- Keep each domain, Conversation, document, and runtime under its exact owner across navigation and reopen.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `desktop-workbench-scene-composition`

### Modified Capabilities

<!-- None. -->

## Impact

Host owns Window navigation and Scene composition; package owners retain business facts and runtime lifecycle. Existing Projects, Conversations, documents, and tasks remain preserved.
