# Reference-video Method Adaptation

## 中文指南

仅在创作者获得授权分析参考视频，并希望把方法迁移到不同内容时使用。先明确全片或时间范围、剪辑点、代表帧、对白/转录、声音线索和未检查区间；缩略图或少量未标注画面不能代表整部作品风格。

提取可迁移的开场钩子、信息顺序、镜头时长/剪辑密度、景别与机位分布、调度/表演/转场、光色材质与声音角色。把这些方法与来源特有角色、对白、动作设计、地点、品牌和标志性构图区分开，并用创作者授权的叙事、角色和资产替换后者。不承诺精确复制特定创作者风格；最小结果是方法画像加一个映射示例或下一决定，并标明证据、解释和未验证项。

## English guidance

Use this guide when the creator is authorized to analyze a reference video and wants to transfer its method to different content.

Establish evidence coverage first: full video or selected timestamp range, cut points, representative frames, transcript/dialogue, sound cues and any unreviewed intervals. Do not infer an entire work's style from one thumbnail or a few unlabelled frames.

Extract transferable rules rather than protected content:

- opening hook and information order;
- shot duration range, cut density and pacing changes;
- shot-size and camera-position distribution;
- blocking, performance and transition patterns;
- light, palette, texture and graphic treatment;
- dialogue, ambience, music, silence and sound-transition roles;
- how the method changes for action, dialogue, reveal or emotional release.

Separate rules that serve the creator's goal from source-specific characters, dialogue, choreography, locations, branding, distinctive compositions and other protected expression. Replace the latter with the creator's authorized narrative, characters, assets and brand system. Do not promise exact replication of a named creator's style; describe observable techniques and the intended adaptation effect.

The smallest useful result is a method profile plus one mapped example or next decision. Produce alternatives, a complete shot table or an execution plan only when requested or needed to resolve a material tradeoff. State which rules are evidence-backed, which are interpretations and which remain unverified.
